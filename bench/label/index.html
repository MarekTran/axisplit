Receipt labels

How to label
  • One entry per priced line, top to bottom. Select it, then click every OCR line that belongs to it: name, wrapped name, quantity line and price.
  • item: something bought. modifier: an add-on or change printed for an item ("+ Cheese", "extra shot", "no onion", even at 0,00). deposit: bottle or packaging deposit. discount: a negative line. fee: service, tip or delivery that's part of the total.
  • Modifiers and deposits say which item they're for; a discount does too when it's for one item.
  • Price = the line total as printed. Qty and unit only as printed: leave them empty when the receipt doesn't print them. A printed "1" is a quantity.
  • Total = the amount to pay as printed. Not the tip on a card slip, not the VAT table.
  • OCR missed text? Shift+drag on the photo draws a box for the selected entry; click a drawn box to remove it.
